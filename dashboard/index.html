How the value is worked out

Implied value = TVL × 0.30 (a token usually lists near a third of TVL) × launch odds × growth boost × 0.12 (the share of the listing value a farmer can expect). Launch odds follow age: 0.30 at birth, rising to 0.80 at two years old, back to 0.30 past four years — a protocol that has run two years without a token is at peak launch pressure. Growth boost is 1 + the 7-day TVL change (clamped to −40%…+100%) × 0.004. The board covers the 40-protocol watchlist (TVL at or above $50M, no token) plus every rankable pre-token protocol below that floor.
