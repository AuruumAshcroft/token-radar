How the value is worked out

Implied value is the total farmer pool for a protocol: its market-implied listing value × launch odds × growth boost × 0.12, the share of the listing value a farmer can expect — the whole pool for every farmer, not one wallet's share. The listing value is TVL × the market multiple for the protocol's business model × a size factor (1B / TVL)^0.3, so a protocol ten times bigger is not worth ten times more. The multiple is the median live mcap/TVL among live protocols in the same category in this snapshot (at least five of them trading; the all-live median 0.17 otherwise), clamped to 0.05…0.60 — Dexs 0.34, RWA 0.27, Lending 0.13, Yield 0.12, Yield Aggregator 0.05, Basis Trading 0.60 (capped). Launch odds follow age: 0.30 at birth, rising to 0.80 at two years old, back to 0.30 past four years — a protocol that has run two years without a token is at peak launch pressure. Growth boost is 1 + the 7-day TVL change (clamped to −40%…+100%) × 0.008. The board covers the 40-protocol watchlist (TVL at or above $50M, no token) plus every rankable pre-token protocol below that floor.
